Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 6, 2026

1. Overview

SleepZen is a sleep sounds, breathing, meditation, and relaxation app. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

2. Information We Collect

SleepZen does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your name, phone number, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone recordings.

The app may store your app preferences locally on your device, such as onboarding status, selected sounds, custom mixes, timer choices, and playback settings.

3. Audio and Meditation Use

SleepZen plays audio resources included with the app. The app does not record your voice, listen to your environment, or upload your audio activity to our servers.

4. Device and Store Services

When you install or update the app through Google Play or Samsung Galaxy Store, those platforms may process information according to their own privacy policies. SleepZen does not control those platform practices.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your locally stored app preferences with third parties.

6. Data Retention

Local preferences remain on your device until you clear the app data or uninstall the app. Because the app does not require an account, we do not maintain a user profile for you on our servers.

7. Children

SleepZen is intended for general wellness and relaxation. It is not designed to collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if the app changes. The updated version will be shown in the app with a new effective date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the SleepZen developer through the app store listing where you downloaded the app.
