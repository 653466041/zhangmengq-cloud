Assistant Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 19, 2026
Package name: com.zhang.pdftoolkitapp

This Privacy Policy explains how Assistant accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the app.

1. Who We Are

Assistant is a PDF and image toolkit that provides features such as PDF viewing, compression, merging, splitting, encryption, decryption, printing, image to PDF, text to PDF, image watermarking, and long image generation.

Developer: ZHANG MENG
Email: zqiang1678@gmail.com

2. Information We Access or Process

To provide app features, Assistant may access or process the following information after you choose files or grant permissions:

  • Files and documents, including PDFs, DOCX files, and other documents selected by you.
  • Images and media selected by you for image to PDF, watermarking, and long image generation.
  • Generated files, including PDFs, images, and documents created locally on your device.
  • Device and advertising-related information that may be processed by third-party SDKs such as Vungle and Google Play services, including advertising identifiers, device information, app interaction information, crash data, or diagnostics.

3. Local File Processing

Assistant processes PDF and image files mainly on your device. Files you select and files generated by the app are not uploaded to servers operated by the developer.

The app may use local cache directories for temporary files during processing, such as temporary PDF compression output or processing cache. These files are used only to complete the current operation and are usually deleted after completion or failure. They may also be removed by the system when cache is cleared.

4. How We Use Information

  • To open, preview, edit, convert, or generate files selected by you.
  • To save processed files to your device, such as Downloads, Pictures, or app-specific folders.
  • To show ads, prevent ad abuse, and measure ad performance.
  • To maintain app stability, security, and performance.
  • To respond to feedback, troubleshoot errors, and improve the app experience.

5. Permissions

Depending on your Android version and the feature you use, Assistant may request the following permissions:

  • Photos and media access: used to read images selected by you.
  • Read external storage: used on older Android versions to read files selected by you.
  • Write external storage: used on older Android versions to save generated files.
  • Manage external storage: if enabled, it is used only for file-tool operations such as reading, saving, and managing files.

If you deny a permission, some file selection, reading, or saving features may not work properly.

6. Third-Party Services

Assistant integrates third-party services for advertising and platform support. These third parties may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies.

  • Vungle SDK: used to show ads and may process advertising identifiers, device information, app usage information, and diagnostics.
  • Google Play services: used to support Google-related service capabilities.

Related policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Vungle Privacy Policy

7. Sharing

We do not sell your PDFs, images, or personal files. We do not actively share file contents with third parties except in the following cases:

  • You choose to share a file with another app or service through Android system sharing.
  • Third-party SDKs process necessary device, advertising, or diagnostic information to provide ads or maintain services.
  • Disclosure is required by applicable laws, regulations, courts, or government authorities.

8. Retention and Deletion

Your source files usually remain on your device or with the file provider you choose. Assistant does not copy them to developer-operated servers. Files generated by the app are saved on your device, and you can delete them through your file manager, gallery, or system settings.

You can clear app cache and temporary files through system app storage settings, or uninstall the app to remove local app data. Data processed by third-party SDKs is retained and deleted according to those third parties' policies.

9. Children's Privacy

Assistant is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will handle it within a reasonable scope.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data during local processing. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be absolutely secure. You should protect your device, files, and accounts.

11. Your Choices

  • You can choose not to select specific files or images.
  • You can revoke app permissions in system settings.
  • You can delete generated files, clear cache, or uninstall the app.
  • You can reset or limit the advertising identifier in system advertising settings.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy due to feature changes, legal requirements, or Google Play policy requirements. When updated, the effective date on this page will be changed. Major changes may be notified in the app or by other reasonable means.

Assistant 是一款 PDF 与图片处理工具，提供 PDF 阅读、压缩、合并、拆分、加密、解密、打印、图片转 PDF、文本转 PDF、图片水印、长图生成等功能。

2. 我们访问或处理的信息

为了提供应用功能，Assistant 可能会在用户主动选择文件或授权后访问或处理以下信息：

  • 文件和文档：用户选择的 PDF、DOCX 或其他文档文件。
  • 图片和媒体：用户选择的图片，用于图片转 PDF、添加水印、生成长图等功能。
  • 生成的文件：应用在设备本地生成的 PDF、图片或文档输出文件。
  • 设备和广告相关信息：通过 Vungle、Google Play 服务等第三方 SDK 可能处理设备标识符、广告标识符、设备信息、应用交互信息、崩溃或诊断信息等。

3. 文件本地处理

Assistant 的 PDF 和图片处理功能主要在用户设备本地完成。用户选择的 PDF、图片和应用生成的文件不会上传到开发者运营的服务器。

应用可能会在处理过程中使用设备本地缓存目录保存临时文件，例如 PDF 压缩时的临时输出文件或处理缓存。这些临时文件仅用于完成当前操作，通常会在操作完成或失败后删除，也可能由系统在清理缓存时删除。

4. 我们如何使用信息

  • 打开、预览、编辑、转换或生成用户选择的文件。
  • 将处理后的文件保存到用户设备的下载、图片或应用指定目录。
  • 展示广告、限制广告滥用、统计广告效果。
  • 维护应用稳定性、安全性和性能。
  • 响应用户反馈、排查错误或改进产品体验。

5. 权限说明

Assistant 可能请求以下 Android 权限，具体取决于系统版本和用户使用的功能：

  • 图片和媒体访问权限：用于读取用户选择的图片。
  • 读取外部存储权限：用于在旧版 Android 系统上读取用户选择的文件。
